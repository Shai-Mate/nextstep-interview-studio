NextStep

How your data stays private

Your words stay yours.

NextStep is free interview practice for any job. Everything you type stays with you.

Everything stays on this deviceThe job ads you paste, your notes and your practice answers are saved in this browser, on this device. They are never uploaded. Anyone who can open this browser on this device can see them. On iPhone, Safari can clear a website's saved data if you don't visit for about a week. Added to your Home Screen, NextStep keeps your work.
No account, no trackingThere is nothing to sign up for. No analytics, no ads, no trackers. GitHub, which hosts the app, gets the normal requests any website gets for its files, never what you type.
No AI callsReading the job ad, building your plan and the feedback on your answers all happen on your phone, using plain rules. Nothing you write is sent to an AI service.
Practising out loud stays private tooIf you choose to practise out loud, the recording is kept in memory only while you are on that question, so you can play it back. It is never saved or sent anywhere, and it is gone when you move on. Read-aloud uses only voices built into your phone.
Works offlineAfter your first visit, the app keeps working without internet, even in airplane mode.
Back up or delete, any timeIn the app, tap On this device on the first screen, or Privacy and your data under Progress. There you'll find Export backup, Restore a backup and Delete all data. A backup file contains your answers, so keep it private. Clearing this site's data in your browser also deletes everything, and on iPhone removing NextStep from your Home Screen can too.
Feedback is publicIf you send feedback on GitHub, anyone can read it. Please don't paste private answers or personal details.
