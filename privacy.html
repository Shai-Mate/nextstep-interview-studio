Next Step

How your data stays private

Your answers stay yours.

Next Step is free interview practice for any job. Your notes and practice answers stay on your phone.

Answers stay on this phoneNotes and practice answers are saved in this browser, on this device. They are not uploaded. Anyone who can open this browser on this device can see them. On iPhone, Safari can clear a website's saved data if you don't visit for about a week. Added to your Home Screen, Next Step keeps your work.
A job link is read for youWhen you paste a job link, that link and the text of the public page are sent to our server (a small Cloudflare Worker) so we can read the page. The company, the role and the requirements are taken from that text. We do not keep the link or the page after the request. If the page cannot be read, for example a sign-in wall on LinkedIn or Indeed, you paste the ad instead. Pasted text stays on this phone until you choose a personal course.
A personal course sends the ad and About youAbout you (a résumé, a LinkedIn profile, or a few answers) stays on this phone. It is sent only when you tap Write my course, and only to the tower address you saved under Personal course. The job ad goes with it. The tower passes them to Codex for that request and does not keep them. Notes and answer-bank drafts are never sent. The shared secret stays on this phone and is not included in an exported backup. If you skip About you, or My subscription is not set up, or the course does not come back clean, the lessons are the general ones, built on this phone, and About you is not sent.
No account, no trackingThere is nothing to sign up for. No analytics, no ads, no trackers. GitHub, which hosts the app, gets the normal requests any website gets for its files. The practice grade is worked out on your phone from a checklist. It is not a hiring verdict, and it is not sent anywhere.
A live practice interviewIf you tap Practice the interview while My subscription is connected, the questions and the words you say or type are sent to your tower for that turn, then the debrief. The tower does not keep them. The text of the session is saved on this phone so you can read it later. The audio is not saved. If you are not connected, the interview stays on this phone and uses generic questions from the ad.
Practising out loud stays private tooIf you choose to practise out loud, the recording is kept in memory only while you are on that question, so you can play it back. It is never saved or sent anywhere, and it is gone when you move on. Read-aloud uses only voices built into your phone.
Works offline after the first visitPractice keeps working without internet, even in airplane mode. Reading a new job link needs a connection. A new personal course needs a connection to your tower. The general lessons still build offline from a pasted ad or a sample ad.
Back up or delete, any timeIn the app, tap On this device on the first screen, or Privacy and your data under Progress. There you'll find Export backup, Restore a backup and Delete all data. A backup file contains your answers, so keep it private. Clearing this site's data in your browser also deletes everything, and on iPhone removing Next Step from your Home Screen can too.
Feedback is publicIf you send feedback on GitHub, anyone can read it. Please don't paste private answers or personal details.
