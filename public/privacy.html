Momentum is a career-preparation app by Prism. Your career story belongs to you. Here is what the app saves, when information can leave your device, and how you stay in control.

What Momentum saves

Momentum saves information you add to your workspace: candidate profile and résumé text, job descriptions and links, company and interviewer notes, application drafts, interview answers, learning progress and offer-preparation details. This information lets you resume your work and tailor it to an opportunity.

In the core experience, this information is processed and stored on your device. Momentum does not create an account, run advertising or analytics trackers, or automatically upload your preparation to a cloud account.

You choose which files to import. Only use information you have permission to use, and leave out passwords, government identifiers, payment details and sensitive information that is not needed for career preparation.

When information leaves your device

Online assistance is optional and may not be available in your edition. You can use pasted job text and local preparation without it.

  • Importing a public job link. When you request an online import, the configured import service receives the URL and requests that page from its publisher. The publisher receives the service's request. The link can contain identifiers or tracking information; remove private tokens before importing. This does not sign in to a job platform or grant access to your accounts.
  • Optional AI assistance. If enabled, the app asks permission before sending the relevant job description, candidate profile, question, answer or supplied context to its configured service and OpenAI. The consent screen explains the information involved. OpenAI processes the request to generate the assistance you requested. You can decline and continue using local preparation.
  • Optional public professional research. If this separate feature is available and you choose it, the job description and supplied company/interviewer research context are sent to OpenAI. Professional names, organizations, roles and related public-work terms may be included in queries shared with its search providers. Your candidate profile and practice answers are excluded from this research request. Keep research notes focused on public professional information and review the linked sources.
  • Exports and sharing. If you export or share your work, the file or text goes to the destination you choose. Your selected app or storage provider handles it under its own privacy terms.
  • Reading a question aloud. If you use this control, the practice question is passed to your browser or operating system's speech service. Voice availability and processing depend on your platform and settings. Momentum does not record your microphone or camera.
  • External links and support. Opening an employer site, source page or support link sends a normal request to that site. Information you submit there is governed by that site's policy.

The optional Momentum service does not save a copy of your request. Hosting and AI providers may still retain connection information or submitted content for security and other purposes described in their policies. This is not a promise of zero provider retention. See OpenAI's API data information before choosing online assistance.

When you access a hosted version of Momentum, its web host receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address, requested app files and browser information to deliver and secure the site. This is separate from your locally saved preparation.

How long your work stays

Your local work remains until you remove it through Momentum's data controls, clear the app or browser's data, or uninstall the app. Private browsing, device storage limits and platform cleanup can also affect saved work. Momentum does not independently encrypt local records or exported backups. Someone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to read them.

Your device's operating system may include app data in a device backup, depending on your settings. A browser, a Home Screen installation and the native app may have separate storage. Keep a backup before switching installations or clearing data.

Exporting and deleting your data

Use the app's data controls to export a backup, restore a saved backup or delete local data. A backup may include personal career information and interview answers. Store it somewhere private and share only the information you intend to share.

On iOS, sharing an export creates a temporary file in the app's cache. Momentum attempts to remove it when sharing finishes or is cancelled. If the app closes during sharing, that temporary copy may remain until the app or operating system clears it.

Deleting local data does not delete files you already exported, operating-system backups, information you submitted to another site, or data previously sent to an online provider. Manage those copies with the relevant provider. With local-only preparation, Momentum does not hold a server copy of your workspace and cannot retrieve it for you.

There is no Momentum account to close in the current local-first app. You can stop using optional online assistance at any time; this prevents future requests but does not undo earlier requests.

Questions and changes

Visit Momentum support for help with data controls and technical questions. Do not put your résumé, passwords, interview answers or other private information in a public support report.

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. If an update introduces new online services or changes how your information is used, the app will explain those changes before you choose to use them.

One useful habit: export a private backup before changing devices, browsers or installations. Local storage is convenient, but it is not a backup service.
