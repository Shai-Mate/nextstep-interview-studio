Common questions

Which devices and browsers are supported?

The iPhone release targets iOS 18 or later. Use a current Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari browser for the web edition; PDF import requires Safari 18 or later on Apple devices. If a document will not import, paste its text instead. Check the App Store listing for the requirements of the published native release.

Do I need an account or a connected job profile?

No. Core preparation works without a Momentum account. Momentum does not ask for your LinkedIn or Indeed password, edit those profiles automatically, or submit applications on your behalf. You can prepare materials in Momentum and use them yourself when applying.

A job link will not import. What should I do?

Some job sites require a login, block automated requests or show their content only after the page runs in a browser. Online import also needs an available configured service. Open the listing yourself, copy the role description, responsibilities and requirements, and paste that text into Momentum. Keep the original URL as a reference.

Why doesn't my document import correctly?

Use a PDF with selectable text, a Word .docx file, or a text/Markdown file. Files must be no larger than 5 MB, and PDFs must have 35 pages or fewer. Older .doc files, password-protected PDFs and scanned images are not supported. A Word document with large embedded images may also exceed the import limits.

Open the document and copy its relevant text into Momentum instead, or save a smaller supported copy. Always review extracted text before saving.

Does Momentum research the interviewer for me?

The local research workspace helps you organize company and interviewer context from information you supply. Add useful public professional details, source links and your questions. If optional public web research is connected, you can separately agree to a search for public professional sources. That feature shares the listed job and research details with OpenAI and may share professional search terms with its search providers.

Source links help you check a brief; they do not prove every statement is correct or that two people with the same name are the same person. Confirm identity, dates and important details with the original source. Momentum does not inspect private accounts or predict an interviewer's personality or hiring decision.

Is the interview feedback a hiring score?

No. Local feedback checks the structure of your practice answer: context, your contribution, an outcome and clarity. It does not certify technical correctness or predict whether an employer will hire you. Use it to improve an answer, then check the facts and practise in your own voice.

Can I practise out loud?

Yes. Say your answer aloud, then type its key points to save and review it. Where your browser supports it, Read question aloud uses your device's speech service to speak the prompt. Available voices and offline support depend on your device. Momentum does not record your microphone or camera.

Where is my progress saved?

Your profile, opportunities, notes and practice progress use local storage in this installation. They do not automatically sync to another phone, browser or app installation. Clearing site data, uninstalling or private browsing can remove saved work. Export a backup from the app's data controls before switching devices or clearing anything.

How do I move my work to another device?

Open Settings & privacy, export a backup and save it privately. On the other device, open the same settings and choose the backup file. Restoring replaces the workspace on that device after confirmation; it does not merge workspaces. Export any existing work there first if you want to keep it. A backup includes your private preparation, so avoid public folders and shared computers.

How many practice rounds and opportunities can I keep?

Each opportunity keeps your current practice round and the latest 10 previous rounds. Starting another round replaces the oldest saved round once that limit is reached. Export a workspace backup before restarting if you want to preserve older history.

A workspace supports up to 100 opportunities and 100 offers. When you reach a limit, export a backup and remove an older item before adding another. Archiving an opportunity keeps it in your workspace and still counts toward the limit.

How do I keep useful AI suggestions?

If optional online coaching is connected, use the save control shown with the response, or copy or export it. Saved coaching notes are stored on your device and included in a workspace backup. They remain suggestions to review, not verified facts or instructions you must follow.

Each opportunity or offer can hold up to 12 saved coaching notes; larger notes may reach the space limit sooner. If saving is blocked, export the response or remove a note you no longer need. Closing an online request cannot recall information already sent to the provider.

The app says it could not save. Is my work safe?

A save warning means local storage may be full, blocked or unavailable. Keep the app open and export a backup immediately if you can. Copy any important unsaved text to a private document before reloading, closing the app or changing browser settings. Do not assume a failed save will recover itself.

Can I use Momentum offline?

The installed app's local preparation workflows are designed for offline use. The web edition needs to finish loading its app files before it is available offline. Importing a public website, opening an external source or using optional online assistance needs a connection.

How do I delete my information?

Open the app's data controls and choose the relevant deletion option. Read its confirmation to understand the scope. This removes active local data, but it does not delete exported backups, operating-system backups or information you have already shared with another service. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can Momentum guarantee an interview or an offer?

No. Momentum supports your preparation and organization; employers make their own decisions. Offer tools help you compare your inputs and prepare questions, but do not verify salary-market data or replace individual financial or legal advice.

Something still isn't working?

Report a technical issue in Momentum's project support tracker. Tell us your device, operating-system version, what you were trying to do and what happened. A short set of steps helps make the issue reproducible.
